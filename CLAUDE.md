# bir-curry-reference

Single-file project: `index.html` is the BIR Curry System reference (base gravy, mix powder, house tikka, decoder, full recipes). v3 as of 28/07/2026, the **differentiation rewrite**: Douglas's verdict was that the decoder's per-curry diffs were too timid, so every dish converged on "house curry". v3 amplifies every diff to full takeaway volume: per-dish heat in house-jar terms (two-jar rule: Kashmiri for colour + a HOT jar for Madras upward), explicit sauce/consistency targets per dish, loud sweet-sour quantities (Pathia 2½-3 tbsp tamarind, Dhansak 250 g lentils + 1 tbsp sugar + 2 tbsp lemon), Vindaloo vinegar-garlic doubled and cooked in early, Ceylon 200 ml coconut + whole lime, Pasanda pushed apart from Korma (nut-led vs coconut-sweet), Dopiaza two-onions-two-ways, and a flavour-cluster map for picking distant pairs on two-curry nights. Same day: tikka batch rescaled to the reality Douglas cooks - **650 g (one breast pack) is the canonical batch**, yielding the 550 g pre-cooked one serves-4 curry needs (one batch = one curry; the 1.5 kg version is retired as "party batch = x2⅓"), and the marinade's vegetable oil swapped to light olive (house seed-oil rule). (v2 11/06/2026: house standards, corrected tikka marinade, realistic yields, vegetable curry, mango chutney in the Saag/balti entry.)
